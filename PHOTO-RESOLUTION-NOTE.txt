WEB-RESOLUTION PHOTO POLICY

These are intentionally reduced-resolution public derivatives.

Feature images:
- maximum long edge: 1400 px
- quality: ~80–82

Photography gallery:
- maximum long edge: 1200 px
- quality: ~78

They remain clear for normal website viewing on phones and laptops, but are materially less useful for printing, cropping, or commercial reuse than the full-resolution originals.

All public copies retain:
- visible © Holly Harris mark
- copyright/artist metadata
- no original GPS/device metadata

Keep full-resolution originals outside the public GitHub repository.
